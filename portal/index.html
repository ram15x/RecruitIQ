You will answer a few questions by typing. There is no camera or microphone, and nothing is recorded except what is listed here.

The questions can be read aloud to you, and you can answer by speaking. You can also type any answer, or every answer, and that is never held against you.

If you speak: your answer is recorded as audio so that a person can listen to it. Your browser may turn your speech into text on the screen. In Chrome and Edge that is done by a speech service run by Google or Microsoft, and you can correct the text before you submit. No software scores your voice, face or accent, and nothing is judged automatically.

Prefer not to be recorded? Leave the box empty and press "Start by typing only".

What this page also records: your answers, how many times you switch to another tab, how many times you paste text, and how long you take. A person reads these next to your answers. They are not used on their own to judge you, and you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

Short, specific answers about your real work are better than long general ones.
