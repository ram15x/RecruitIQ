# Responsible use

This tool ranks resumes. It does not decide who gets a job.

- A person reads the evidence and writes a reason for every decision. The app enforces this.
- Do not use the score as a cut-off to reject people automatically. In many places automated rejection of candidates is regulated, and the weights here have not been validated against real hiring outcomes.
- Tell candidates that software helps to organise applications, and what data you keep and for how long. The consent text and the 180-day default are placeholders to replace with advice for where you operate.
- Look at the fairness report each hiring round, for the human decisions and for the model-only view. If a group is flagged, look at the process, the job description and the weights. A clean report does not prove the process is fair, and groups smaller than the minimum size are not judged at all.
- Group labels are voluntary and are kept apart from the candidate record. Do not use them for anything except the report.
- The interview page records tab switches, paste counts and time spent. These are facts for a human to read with context. Someone can switch tabs for a good reason. Do not treat them as proof of cheating.
- Rotate any key or password that was ever stored in an earlier version of a project, even if you believe it was never published.
